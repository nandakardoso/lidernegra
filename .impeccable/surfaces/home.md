## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The page extends LÍDERNEGRA's own existing capacitação-deck identity onto the web — a confident editorial-institutional register (deep wine ground, gold rule-work, italic display serif) rather than the generic NGO cream-and-terracotta default this run nearly shipped instead.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep wine/burgundy (#5C1330-ish) as the dominant ground for hero and section-break panels, warm gold/mustard (#C9A227-ish) as the sole accent (rules, kickers, numerals, CTA underlines), cream/off-white (#F7F1E8-ish) as the light-surface ground for reading sections, near-black (#211012-ish) for body text on cream. Display serif with elegant italic character for headlines and pull quotes (transitional/Didone-adjacent, editorial register), paired with a clean humanist sans for body/UI text. Recurring motifs carried over from the brand's real materials: thin gold arcing rule fragments in dark-panel corners, a solid-fill circular numeral badge (wine circle, gold number) for the 5-stage journey, a thin gold horizontal rule under kickers, generous negative space.

**STORY:** Visitor arrives on a wine-ground hero that states the program's real identity and slogan, reads the manifesto-like premise, moves through the real 5-stage Jornada (not a generic 5-step funnel), sees the permanent Comunidade layer as a parallel pillar (not an afterthought), meets mentoras/mentoradas as editorial profiles, sees impact figures (placeholders), and is moved to inscrever-se or become a parceiro institucional.
